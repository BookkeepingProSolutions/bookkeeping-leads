## 📧 Email Response Template

When someone replies to your cold email:

```
Hi [Name],

Great to hear from you! I'd love to learn more about [Company Name]'s financial setup and see if I can help.

Are you available for a quick 15-minute call this week?

Here are some times that work for me:
- Tuesday at 10am or 2pm
- Wednesday at 11am or 3pm
- Thursday at 9am or 1pm

Or feel free to suggest a time that works better for you.

Looking forward to chatting!

Best,
[Your Name]
[Phone]
```

## 📞 Consultation Script

### Opening (2 min)
"Thanks for taking the time to chat! I reviewed your website/LinkedIn and [specific compliment]. Tell me about your business - how long have you been operating?"

### Discovery Questions (8 min)
1. "How are you currently handling your bookkeeping?"
2. "What are your biggest frustrations with your current setup?"
3. "Are you tracking job profitability in real-time?"
4. "How do you handle tax planning - quarterly or year-end?"
5. "What are your goals for the business in the next 12 months?"

### Solution (3 min)
"Based on what you've shared, here's how I can help..."
- Specific solution to their pain points
- Expected outcomes
- Timeframe

### Next Steps (2 min)
"I'll send you a proposal with pricing and next steps. Does that sound good?"

Get email confirmation, send proposal same day.

## 💡 Pro Tips

**Personalization Matters:**
- Mention a specific project they've done
- Reference their LinkedIn posts
- Show you did research

**Respond Quickly:**
- Reply to leads within 1 hour
- Fast response = higher close rate

**Follow Up Relentlessly:**
- Most deals close after 3-5 touchpoints
- Don't give up after one email

**Track Everything:**
- Update spreadsheet daily
- Review weekly
- Optimize based on data
