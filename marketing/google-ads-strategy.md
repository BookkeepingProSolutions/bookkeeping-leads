# Google Ads Campaign Strategy Guide
## Multi-Niche Bookkeeping Lead Generation Strategy

This guide provides the complete blueprint for launching and optimizing a high-converting Google Ads campaign targeting our new dynamic landing page. 

By utilizing our query parameter support (`?niche=...`), you can run separate campaigns for each industry and drive clicks directly to a pre-selected landing page that matches the searcher's specific bookkeeping needs and visual branding.

---

## 🎯 Quick Setup Checklist

- [ ] **Link GA4 to Google Ads** (Admin → Product Links → Google Ads Links).
- [ ] **Import Conversions**: Import `lead_magnet_download`, `form_submission`, and `calendly_click` from GA4 as conversion actions in Google Ads.
- [ ] **Create 4 Campaigns** (or 1 Campaign with 4 Ad Groups). We recommend **4 separate campaigns** so you can allocate budgets independently based on niche performance and profitability.
- [ ] **Configure UTM Tracking**: Add the tracking template to capture source, medium, campaign, and keywords.
- [ ] **Define Negative Keywords**: Add standard negative list to block jobs, courses, and software searches.

---

## 📁 Campaign Structure Overview

To maximize quality scores and minimize cost-per-click (CPC), structure your Google Ads account as follows:

```
[Google Ads Account]
    ├── Campaign 1: Construction Bookkeeping (Target: /?niche=construction)
    ├── Campaign 2: Legal / Law Firm Bookkeeping (Target: /?niche=lawyer)
    ├── Campaign 3: Real Estate Bookkeeping (Target: /?niche=real-estate)
    └── Campaign 4: Medical / Clinic Bookkeeping (Target: /?niche=medical)
```

## 🔗 UTM Tracking Template

Add this template to the **Tracking Template** field in Campaign Settings:

```
{lpurl}?utm_source=google&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign={campaignid}&utm_content={creative}&utm_term={keyword}
```

This ensures that Google Analytics automatically tracks which keywords and ads drove every ebook download, form submission, and Calendly call.
