Medical Practice Tax Minimization Checklist

A Strategic Guide for Clinical Practices and Doctors

Medical practice owners typically overpay on taxes by $30,000 - $80,000 per year. Missed hardware write-offs, unoptimized clinical entity classifications, and overlooked research credits are primary causes.

1. Equipment & Clinic Write-Offs

  • Write off clinical hardware (imaging, diagnostic devices, exam chairs, computer terminals) immediately using Section 179.
  • Deduct EHR and medical practice software subscription fees.
  • Claim research tax credits (R&D) if your clinic engages in custom treatment methodologies, patient trials, or medical studies.
  • Deduct professional liability & malpractice insurance premiums.
01

2. Personnel & Travel Deductions

  • Deduct Continuing Medical Education (CME) expenses, licensing fees, and related travel and lodging.
  • Establish an Accountable Plan to reimburse owners and clinical staff tax-free for home workspace, mobile plans, and mileage.
  • Optimize pension and retirement contributions (like Safe Harbor 401ks or cash balance plans) to shield practice income.
