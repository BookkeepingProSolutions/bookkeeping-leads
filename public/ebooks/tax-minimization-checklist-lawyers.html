Law Firm Tax Minimization Checklist

A Strategic Guide for Attorneys and Law Firms

Law firm owners typically overpay on taxes by $20,000 - $60,000 per year due to SSTB income limitations, missed office write-offs, or poorly structured partner compensations.

1. Legal Entity & QBI Optimization

  • Evaluate S-Corporation Election to reduce partner exposure to self-employment taxes.
  • Monitor SSTB threshold limitations to protect and maximize your Qualified Business Income (QBI) deduction.
  • Establish defined benefit/retirement structures (like cash balance plans) to lower partner net taxable income while building personal wealth.
01

2. Operating & Client Cost Deductions

  • Track legal practice software fees (Clio, Smokeball, MyCase) as deductible operating expenses.
  • Deduct marketing and client acquisition costs (websites, directories, local listings).
  • Reconcile Advanced Client Costs to ensure they are handled properly on the balance sheet and not accidentally omitted as write-offs.
  • Claim home office deductions and establish an Accountable Plan for cell, internet, and travel.
