Real Estate Business Tax Minimization Checklist

A Strategic Guide for Investors and Agents

Real estate business owners typically overpay on taxes by $10,000 - $40,000 per year.

Missed depreciation categories, unallocated travel expenses, and poor entity setups are common culprits. Use this checklist to audit your books and maximize your savings.

1. Property-Level Deductions

  • Claim accelerated depreciation on equipment and building improvements using cost segregation studies.
  • Deduct property insurance, property taxes, and mortgage interest per property.
  • Track repairs and maintenance separately from capital improvements (which must be depreciated).
  • Deduct advertising, tenant screening, property management fees, and professional commissions.
Pro Tip: Keep repairs under the $2,500 Safe Harbor election threshold to write them off in full immediately.
01

2. Travel & Vehicle Tracking

  • Maintain a detailed mileage log for property showings, listing appointments, supply runs, and property management visits.
  • Deduct actual vehicle expenses (fuel, insurance, lease, depreciation) or standard mileage rate.
  • Deduct travel costs for out-of-town property inspections, education seminars, and real estate conferences.
