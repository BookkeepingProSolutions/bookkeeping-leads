Healthcare providers dedicate their careers to patient care. Unfortunately, managing clinical overhead, insurer reimbursements, and complex healthcare tax codes can lead to major profit leaks.

In this guide, we detail 7 practical financial strategies that medical practices, dental clinics, and wellness providers use to capture more profits, protect compliance, and streamline operations.

01

1Optimize Corporate Entity Setup

Estimated Savings: $20,000 – $50,000/year

Depending on your state laws, medical practices must operate as Professional Corporations (PCs) or Professional LLCs. Choosing an S-Corp election within these entities can heavily mitigate self-employment tax exposure on practice earnings.

02

2Accelerate Medical Equipment Depreciation

Estimated Savings: $15,000 – $80,000/year

Utilize Section 179 and bonus depreciation to write off 100% of the cost of expensive clinical hardware, imaging systems, dental chairs, and IT networks in the year they are placed in service.

03

3Audit Overhead & Supply Chains

Estimated Savings: $10,000 – $25,000/year

Medical supplies, medical waste, and pharmaceutical products are subject to major pricing fluctuations. Categorize and monitor these costs per practitioner or treatment category to locate margin leaks.

04

4Maximize the R&D Tax Credit

Estimated Savings: $10,000 – $40,000/year

Practices that engage in clinical research, develop custom treatment models, or design specialized healthcare software can qualify for the Credit for Increasing Research Activities, reducing direct income tax liability.

05

5Implement an Accountable Plan

Estimated Savings: $3,000 – $8,000/year

Reimburse medical personnel and owners tax-free for home office workspace, cellular usage, continuing medical education (CME), and clinical travel.

06

6Leverage ins-network Billing Dashboards

Estimated Savings: Reduced Billing Cycle Time

Integrate EHR billing data (such as Athenahealth or eClinicalWorks) with your general ledger. This helps track accounts receivables (A/R) from private insurers and Medicare, preventing aging debt write-offs.

07

7Partner with a HIPAA-Compliant Bookkeeper

Estimated Savings: Compliance Assurance

Financial records must respect patient privacy under HIPAA. Specialized healthcare bookkeeping ensures patient details remain protected while keeping clean, audit-proof books.
