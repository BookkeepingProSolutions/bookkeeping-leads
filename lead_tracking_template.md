## Status Definitions
- **New Lead**: Just downloaded lead magnet or filled form
- **Contacted**: You've reached out via email/phone
- **Consultation Booked**: Call scheduled
- **Consultation Complete**: Call completed
- **Proposal Sent**: Pricing and services sent
- **Negotiating**: Back-and-forth on terms
- **Closed - Won**: New client!
- **Closed - Lost**: Decided not to work together
- **Nurturing**: Not ready now, follow up later

## Weekly Metrics to Calculate

### Conversion Funnel
1. **Leads** = Total new leads this week
2. **Contacted** = Leads you reached out to
3. **Consultations Booked** = Calls scheduled
4. **Consultations Held** = Calls completed
5. **Proposals Sent** = Pricing sent
6. **Clients Closed** = New clients won

### Conversion Rates
- **Lead → Consultation**: (Consultations Booked / Leads) × 100
  - Target: 20-40%
- **Consultation → Proposal**: (Proposals Sent / Consultations Held) × 100
  - Target: 70-90%
- **Proposal → Close**: (Clients Closed / Proposals Sent) × 100
  - Target: 25-40%
- **Overall Close Rate**: (Clients Closed / Leads) × 100
  - Target: 5-15%

### Revenue Metrics
- **Average Deal Size** = Total Monthly Value / Clients Closed
- **Pipeline Value** = Sum of all proposals sent (pending)
- **Monthly Recurring Revenue** = Total of all active clients

## Lead Sources to Track

- **Website (Organic)**: Direct traffic to site
- **LinkedIn**: Posts, comments, DMs
- **Cold Email**: Outreach campaigns
- **Facebook Groups**: Community engagement
- **Referrals**: From existing clients
- **Google Ads**: When you start running ads
- **Local Networking**: Chamber, events, etc.

Track which sources convert best and double down!

## Follow-Up Schedule

**New Lead:**
- Day 1: Send welcome email immediately
- Day 2: Follow-up email with additional value
- Day 4: Call if they haven't responded
- Day 7: Final email before moving to nurture

**Post-Consultation (No Proposal Yet):**
- Same day: Send summary email
- Day 3: Send proposal
- Day 5: Follow up on proposal
- Day 10: Final follow-up

**Proposal Sent:**
- Day 3: Check in via email
- Day 7: Phone call follow-up
- Day 14: Last attempt before closing file
